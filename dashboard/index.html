Token Launch Radar

198 protocols that record no token, ranked by implied value. Snapshot 14 Nov 2026, 758 protocols. A protocol is said to record no token, never that it has none.

Value if a token launches comes from a model fitted on the 128 traded tokens in this snapshot: log market cap on log TVL plus the terms that lowered the held-out miss in both tests. Implied value = value if it launches × token odds, where the odds are the share of peers that record a token, by how long they ran without one. No protocol name is special-cased.

Verdicts in the board are at the $1,000 reference deposit, exactly as board.json states them; the deposit control scales the same formula and never a second one. The farm economics, one shared formula: fees = $60 + $15 × min(3, number of chains the protocol runs on); airdrop allocations go per active wallet, not pro-rata to TVL, so farmers as a group are assumed to receive 5% of the launch value split across 10,000 active wallets, scaled linearly with the deposit up to 10x the $1,000 reference; net = implied value × 0.05 / 10,000 − fees, over 12 assumed farming hours a year. FARM when net is at least $25 an hour, MARGINAL when it is at least $0, otherwise SKIP. Off-list rows (wrapped, staked or bridged products and custodial exchanges) are never FARM: the radar does not present a product of a token that already trades as worth farming for a new token. Farm steps state what to do, what it costs and how long it takes, and are written for the $1,000 reference deposit. Range honesty is walk-forward: the share of later-listed peers whose realised market cap falls inside the printed P25–P75 range, with the range factors taken from the earlier-listed half only. Nothing here predicts whether or when a token launches.
